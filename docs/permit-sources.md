## Verified Planning Notes

- Tide Avenue is mapped by the County as County-maintained Local Road 000515B-1, from Columbia Avenue to Falcon Cove Road.
- Ordinance 44 requires an approved road approach permit before the building permit, unless Public Works finds an existing approach satisfactory for the proposed use.
- The geohazard submittal checklist requires a signed geologic hazard report, engineer certification that plans conform to the report, designer acknowledgement, stormwater/erosion/grading/vegetation plans, and the approved road approach permit.
- RR-2 has a two-acre minimum; a lot below two acres may be built only if it was legally established before December 18, 2002 and all other requirements are met. Confirm this lot-of-record determination with Community Development.
